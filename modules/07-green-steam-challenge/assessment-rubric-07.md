# Module 7: Green STEAM Challenge

## Assessment Rubric — Sustainability Mini-App

### Competence mapping (DigComp 2.2)

Framework reference: DigComp 2.2. Competence 4.2 is **Protecting personal data and privacy**; engaging in citizenship through digital technologies is competence 2.3.

- 3.1 Developing digital content
- 3.2 Integrating and re-elaborating
- 4.2 Protecting personal data and privacy
- 5.1 Problem solving
- 5.3 Creativity and innovation

Use the same four performance levels across the module rubrics. Score each criterion from 1–4 and record the evidence observed or collected.

| Criterion | Emerging (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem framing and purpose | Problem or user is unclear; purpose is difficult to identify. | Identifies a problem and user, but goal or scope is broad. | Defines a clear user, local problem, and focused app goal. | States constraints and success criteria and explains why the scope fits. | User story, scope notes, or storyboard. |
| Functionality and state | Core interaction is missing or unreliable. | Main interaction partly works; state or feedback has gaps. | Core interaction works and state updates predictably. | Handles relevant edge cases and explains state decisions; persistence is optional. | Working prototype and interaction observation. |
| UX, accessibility, and privacy | Controls or purpose are unclear; privacy/accessibility needs are unaddressed. | Some clear labels or privacy information, but important gaps remain. | Labeled, keyboard-operable controls and clear data/reset information. | Tests with a peer and applies a specific usability, accessibility, or privacy improvement. | Keyboard check, disclosure text, and peer-test note. |
| Reflection and impact communication | Makes unsupported claims or gives little reflection. | Gives a basic explanation but limitations or next steps are vague. | Explains the app’s purpose, limitations, and one improvement; counts are not presented as measured impact. | Uses evidence to assess limitations and proposes a realistic next iteration. | Short reflection and peer feedback note. |
